About us

Engineering partners for ambitious companies.

We are a South African software engineering firm. We build the dependable systems that let a growing business operate without heroics.

Our story

Good businesses are held back by systems that were never designed.

Most owner-led businesses grow by adding tools and people. Eventually the work is held together by inboxes, spreadsheets and the one person who knows how it all connects.


DigitalFoundry exists to replace that with engineered systems that support customers, streamline operations and give leadership clear information.


The name is the idea. A foundry takes raw material and, under controlled conditions, forges something strong and reliable. That is how we treat your business processes.

Our principles

How we do business.

Plain over clever

We choose the simplest solution that does the job and explain it in plain language. If a spreadsheet is enough, we will say so.

You own it

Code, data, configuration and documentation are handed to you. We earn the next engagement by being useful.

People stay in charge

Automation handles the routine. Your people handle judgement, exceptions and relationships.

Measured, not claimed

We baseline before we build and report against the same measures afterwards.

Careful with data

Least-privilege access, encrypted credentials and POPIA-aware handling are standard, not extras.
