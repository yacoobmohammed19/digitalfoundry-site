About us

A foundry for the systems businesses run on.

We are a South African engineering studio. We build the unglamorous plumbing that lets a growing business run without heroics.

Our story

Good businesses are held back by systems that were never designed.

Most owner-led businesses grow by adding tools and people. Eventually the work is held together by inboxes, spreadsheets and one person who knows how it all connects.

DigitalFoundry exists to replace that with engineered systems: AI agents that handle conversations, workflows that move work between tools, and data foundations that everyone trusts.

The name is the idea. A foundry takes raw material and, under controlled conditions, forges something strong and reliable. That is how we treat your processes.

What we believe

Principles we do not negotiate.

  • Plain over cleverWe choose the simplest system that does the job and explain it in plain language. If a spreadsheet is enough, we will say so.
  • You own itCode, data, configuration and documentation are handed to you. We earn the next engagement by being useful, not by being irreplaceable.
  • People stay in chargeAutomation handles the routine path. People handle exceptions, judgement and relationships, and the system makes that hand-off smooth.
  • Measured, not claimedWe baseline before we build and report against the same measures after. We do not publish results we cannot back up.
  • Careful with dataLeast-privilege access, encrypted secrets and POPIA-aware handling are defaults, not extras.
