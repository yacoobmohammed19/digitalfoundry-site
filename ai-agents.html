AI agents

Custom AI agents that work inside your rules.

Customer-facing and internal agents grounded in your approved knowledge, with guardrails, escalation to a named person and full logging.

Overview

Agents that answer from facts you approve, and stop where you tell them to.

An AI agent is not a chatbot bolted onto a website. We design it as a small system: channels it listens on, a knowledge layer it retrieves from, tools it can use, limits it must respect and a person it hands over to.

Every agent is built from your own policies, products and records, so answers stay current and can be traced to a source. Out-of-scope or low-confidence cases never reach the customer unreviewed.

Before launch we build a test set from your real conversations and measure the agent against it. After launch we keep measuring, so quality is a number you can see rather than a feeling.

Where it applies

Typical use cases.

01

After-hours enquiries

Answer, qualify and book leads that arrive when the office is closed, then pass anything unusual to a person with the full thread.

02

Internal knowledge assistant

Staff ask questions about policies, pricing and procedures and get cited answers instead of interrupting a colleague.

03

Document and email triage

Classify incoming mail and attachments, extract key fields and route each item to the right queue.

04

Appointment and order handling

Book, move and confirm appointments or orders through your calendar and systems, with confirmation messages.

05

Payment and follow-up nudges

Polite, accurate reminders on overdue items, with a person stepping in when a customer disputes something.

Multi-channel consistency

One agent serving WhatsApp, web chat and email with the same knowledge and the same limits.
