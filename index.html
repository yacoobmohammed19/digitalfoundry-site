Who we are


Engineering partners for businesses that have outgrown manual work.



About DigitalFoundry →




DigitalFoundry is a South African engineering studio. We take the processes your team keeps patching with spreadsheets, inboxes and copy-paste, and turn them into systems that run on their own.


We work in a fixed four-stage method, Unpack, Prioritise, Forge, Run, with a defined output at the end of every stage. You know what you are getting, what it costs and when it lands before a line of code is written.


Agents act inside limits you set and escalate to a person when it matters. Everything we build is logged, documented and handed over. The code, the data and the documentation are yours.
