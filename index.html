About DigitalFoundry

DigitalFoundry is a South African software engineering firm. We work alongside owner-led and mid-market companies that have outgrown manual processes and disconnected tools, and need dependable technology to support their next stage of growth.


We combine strategic advice with disciplined engineering. Every engagement starts with your business objective, is governed by a clear scope and is measured against outcomes you can verify.

Our approach

A disciplined path from objective to outcome.

Four stages, each with a defined deliverable and a decision point for you.

Discover

We study how your business actually runs, where time and money are lost, and what success looks like in your numbers.

Outcome: a clear business case

Prioritise

We rank the opportunities by return and risk, and agree a fixed scope, milestones and price in writing.

Outcome: signed-off scope and price

Deliver

Our engineers build and test with your team involved, with a working demonstration every two weeks.

Outcome: a tested, documented solution

Support

We launch in stages, train your people, monitor performance and report against the targets we set together.

Outcome: measurable results
